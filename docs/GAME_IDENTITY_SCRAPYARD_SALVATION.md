# SCRAPYARD SALVATION
## Game Identity & Aesthetic Design Document

---

# 🎮 GAME IDENTITY

## Core Concept

**SCRAPYARD SALVATION** is a gritty, industrial top-down survival shooter set in a post-collapse world where humanity clings to existence in fortified scrapyards surrounded by endless wastelands. You are a **Salvager**—a desperate survivor armed with jury-rigged weapons, fighting off waves of mutated scavengers, feral machines, and twisted abominations that crawl from the toxic wastes.

**Tagline:** *"Rust never sleeps. Neither do the horrors."*

---

## The World

### Setting: The Heap

The world ended not with a bang, but with a slow, grinding decay. Industrial collapse. Environmental devastation. The Blight—a mutagenic contamination that twisted flesh and corrupted machines. Now, the only safe havens are the **Heaps**—fortified scrapyards where survivors salvage pre-collapse technology to survive another day.

**Your Heap** is a 50-meter killing ground surrounded by rusted walls of shipping containers and car husks. Beyond the walls, the **Wastes** stretch endlessly—a nightmare landscape of toxic fog, skeletal factories, and roaming horrors.

### Atmosphere Keywords
- **Rust** — Everything is corroded, oxidized, decaying
- **Oil** — Black, viscous, coating everything like blood
- **Smoke** — Perpetual haze from burning waste and distant fires
- **Sparks** — Flickering lights, electrical shorts, welding arcs
- **Grime** — Layers of dirt, grease, and dried organic matter
- **Desperation** — Survival is earned, never guaranteed

# 👤 THE PLAYER: SALVAGER

## Character Identity

The Salvager isn't a hero—they're a **survivor**. Malnourished, scarred, desperate. Their equipment is scavenged, repaired a hundred times, held together with wire and prayer.

### Visual Design

- **Body:** Lean, wiry frame wrapped in layered scrap armor
- **Armor:** Mismatched pieces—car door as shoulder pad, street sign as chest plate, leather straps
- **Mask:** Industrial respirator or welding mask (protects from toxic air)
- **Hands:** Wrapped in dirty bandages, fingerless gloves
- **Silhouette:** Recognizable even as a small sprite—asymmetrical, hunched, ready

### Animation Feel

- **Idle:** Subtle sway, nervous twitching, scanning for threats
- **Move:** Hunched, efficient, conserving energy
- **Shoot:** Recoil kickback, brief muzzle flash glow
- **Hit:** Stagger, brief flash of red, recovery stumble
- **Death:** Collapse, armor pieces scattering, pool of dark liquid

# 👹 THE ENEMIES: HORRORS OF THE WASTES

## Enemy Types (Re-themed)

### 1. CRAWLERS (Former: Chasers)
**Concept:** Mutated humans reduced to feral, animalistic predators. They sprint on all fours, faces twisted into permanent snarls.

- **Appearance:** Emaciated humanoid, elongated limbs, patchy skin, glowing eyes
- **Behavior:** Relentless pursuit, occasional leap attacks
- **Sound:** Chittering, raspy breathing, bone-crack sounds
- **Death:** Crumples, twitches, leaks black ichor

### 2. SHAMBLING HUSKS (Former: Wanderers)
**Concept:** Corpses reanimated by parasitic growths. They stumble aimlessly until prey is sensed, then charge with surprising speed.

- **Appearance:** Bloated, fungal growths erupting from body, one arm grotesquely enlarged
- **Behavior:** Slow patrol → sudden burst charge
- **Sound:** Wet, squelching steps, gurgling moans
- **Death:** Pops with spore cloud, collapses into puddle

### 3. SCRAP SENTINELS (Former: Shooters)
**Concept:** Corrupted pre-collapse security drones. Their targeting systems are damaged, firing in erratic bursts.

- **Appearance:** Hovering drone with exposed wiring, single glowing red eye, rusted chassis
- **Behavior:** Maintains distance, fires toxic projectiles
- **Sound:** Servo whine, static bursts, targeting beeps
- **Death:** Sparks violently, crashes, small explosion

### 4. SWARM RATS (Former: Swarm)
**Concept:** Mutated vermin that attack in overwhelming numbers. Individually weak, terrifying in packs.

- **Appearance:** Rat-like but wrong—too many legs, exposed spine, glowing tumors
- **Behavior:** Flood toward player in groups of 8-12
- **Sound:** Squealing, skittering, hundreds of tiny claws
- **Death:** Squish, splatter, quick fade

### 5. BOSSES

#### THE BEHEMOTH
**Concept:** A massive, bloated horror—once human, now a walking mountain of corrupted flesh and embedded scrap metal.

- **Appearance:** 4x player size, asymmetrical bulk, metal plates fused to skin, multiple vestigial limbs
- **Behavior:** Slow pursuit, devastating ground pounds, spawns Swarm Rats from body
- **Sound:** Deep rumbling roar, metal grinding, wet tearing sounds
- **Death:** Prolonged collapse, explosions of gore, arena-shaking impact

#### THE HARVESTER
**Concept:** A corrupted industrial machine that has developed malevolent intelligence. Part crane, part spider, all nightmare.

- **Appearance:** Mechanical spider legs, dangling hooks, grinding saws, single searchlight "eye"
- **Behavior:** Unpredictable movement, hook grabs, saw attacks, area denial
- **Sound:** Grinding gears, pneumatic hisses, metal-on-metal screeches
- **Death:** Chain explosion, parts flying, final crash

# 🔫 WEAPONS: SCRAP ARSENAL

## Weapon Philosophy

Every weapon is **improvised, unreliable, and deadly**. They look like they could fall apart any second—and they hit like a truck.

### 1. PIPE PISTOL (Starter)
**Concept:** Literal plumbing pipe with a firing mechanism welded on.

- **Appearance:** Copper pipe, exposed springs, tape-wrapped grip, small muzzle flash
- **Sound:** Sharp crack, metallic ping, shell casing tink
- **Feel:** Snappy, reliable, humble

### 2. NAIL SPREADER (Shotgun)
**Concept:** Industrial nail gun modified for combat. Fires bursts of rusted nails.

- **Appearance:** Yellow industrial tool chassis, oversized magazine, duct tape reinforcement
- **Sound:** Pneumatic THUMP, nails whistling, impacts like hail
- **Feel:** Devastating close range, reload vulnerability

### 3. SPARK SPITTER (SMG)
**Concept:** Salvaged car alternator converted to rapid-fire projectile launcher.

- **Appearance:** Automotive parts visible, spinning components, trailing wires, erratic sparks
- **Sound:** Electric crackling, rapid pops, overheating whine
- **Feel:** Spray and pray, overheats if held too long

### 4. BOOM PIPE (Rocket Launcher)
**Concept:** Sewer pipe launching improvised explosives.

- **Appearance:** Large diameter pipe, propane tank warheads, fuse ignition
- **Sound:** WHOOSH launch, whistling flight, BOOM impact
- **Feel:** Slow, devastating, self-damage risk

### 5. THE GRINDER (Melee)
**Concept:** Angle grinder with extended blade, for when ammo runs dry.

- **Appearance:** Spinning disc, sparks flying, blood-stained edge
- **Sound:** Grinding metal, revving motor, screaming impact
- **Feel:** Risky, visceral, desperate

# 🎨 VISUAL EFFECTS

## Gritty Effect Design

### Muzzle Flash
- Asymmetrical, harsh yellow-orange core
- Smoke puff that lingers
- Sparks scattering in random directions
- Brief illumination of nearby surfaces

### Bullet Trails
- Faint smoke trails rather than clean lines
- Occasional tracer rounds (1 in 4) with orange glow
- Hit sparks on metal, dust puffs on ground

### Blood & Gore
- Dark, almost black blood (dried crimson)
- Splatter patterns on ground that persist
- Chunky particles on death
- Blood trails from wounded enemies

### Explosions
- Dirty orange-brown core (not clean fire)
- Black smoke billowing
- Debris shrapnel flying
- Ground scarring/scorch marks remaining

### Environmental Particles (Always Present)
- Drifting ash/dust
- Occasional sparks from damaged equipment
- Toxic fog wisps at arena edges
- Firefly-like embers floating

### Screen Effects
- **Damage:** Red vignette pulse, brief static
- **Low Health:** Persistent dark vignette, heartbeat throb
- **Power-up:** Brief screen tint matching power-up color
- **Boss Spawn:** Screen shake, brief desaturation
- **Kill Streak:** Slight slow-mo on multi-kills (50ms), screen flash

# 🔊 AUDIO IDENTITY

## Soundscape Philosophy

The audio should feel **industrial, mechanical, and oppressive**. Silence is never complete—there's always ambient noise.

### Ambient Soundscape
- Distant machinery grinding
- Wind whistling through metal structures
- Occasional far-off explosions
- Dripping liquids
- Electrical humming and crackling
- Rat squeaks in quiet moments

### Music Direction
- **Gameplay:** Industrial metal, heavy percussion, distorted synths
- **Boss Fights:** Tempo increase, more aggressive drums
- **Wave Complete:** Brief silence, then tension rebuilding
- **Game Over:** Music cuts to oppressive silence, distant sounds

### Sound Effects Style
- **Weapons:** Punchy, mechanical, with metallic resonance
- **Enemies:** Organic horror meets industrial (wet + metal)
- **Impacts:** Heavy, satisfying THUNK and CRUNCH sounds
- **UI:** Typewriter clicks, mechanical switches, CRT static

# 🏗️ ARENA DESIGN

## The Killing Floor

The arena should feel like a **fortified scrapyard staging area**—a space cleared for combat, surrounded by the detritus of survival.

### Arena Elements

#### Floor
- Cracked concrete with oil stains
- Metal plating patches (different rust levels)
- Drain grates with toxic liquid visible below
- Tire marks, scorch patterns, old blood stains

#### Boundaries
- Stacked shipping containers (rusted, dented, graffiti)
- Crushed car wall (various vehicles compressed)
- Chain-link fencing with warning signs
- Toxic fog barrier at certain edges

#### Hazards (Future Feature)
- **Oil Slicks:** Slow movement, flammable
- **Electrical Panels:** Periodic shock bursts
- **Steam Vents:** Obscure vision, minor damage
- **Unstable Ground:** Collapse into hazard zone

#### Props (Visual Only)
- Abandoned vehicles (player cover in future)
- Barrel stacks (explosive in future)
- Workbenches with scattered tools
- Flickering fluorescent lights on poles
- Burning trash cans for light sources

# 🖥️ UI DESIGN

## Interface Philosophy

UI should feel like **scavenged technology**—CRT monitors, analog gauges, hand-scratched labels.

### HUD Elements

#### Health Bar
- Analog gauge style (needle indicator)
- Cracked glass overlay
- Red warning light when low
- Heart icon made of scrap metal

#### Ammo Counter
- LED segment display (like old calculators)
- Flickers occasionally
- Empty shows "---" with warning beep

#### Wave Indicator
- Stamped metal plate appearance
- Scratched numbers
- "WAVE" stenciled military style

#### Score/XP
- Mechanical counter wheels (spinning numbers)
- Brass/bronze metallic finish

#### Power-up Timers
- Analog countdown bars
- Matching power-up color
- Static/glitch as they expire

### Menus

#### Main Menu
- CRT monitor frame around menu
- Scanlines and slight curve distortion
- Flickering text
- Industrial font (Impact, Bebas Neue, or custom stencil)
- Background: slow pan of devastated wasteland

#### Pause Menu
- Translucent dark overlay
- Menu "panel" looks like control console
- Toggle switches for options
- Physical buttons for selections

#### Game Over Screen
- CRT static burst
- "SIGNAL LOST" text
- Slow fade to black
- Stats displayed on "recovery report" clipboard

# 🎯 GAME FEEL ENHANCEMENTS

## Making It Gritty

### Camera Effects
- Subtle constant shake (0.5-1px amplitude)
- Impact shake on damage (scales with damage)
- Boss footstep micro-shakes
- Weapon recoil camera kick
- Death cam: slow zoom out, desaturation

### Visual Polish
- **Chromatic aberration** at screen edges
- **Film grain** overlay (subtle)
- **Vignette** (always present, intensifies when hurt)
- **CRT scanlines** option (subtle)
- **Color grading:** Desaturated, teal-orange pushed

### Gameplay Feel
- **Hit pause:** 20-50ms freeze on hit (enemy and player)
- **Knockback:** Enemies stagger on hit
- **Death reactions:** Enemies ragdoll/crumple uniquely
- **Weapon kick:** Visual recoil, recovery animation
- **Shell casings:** Ejecting, bouncing, fading

### Progression Feel
- XP orbs pulled magnetically (satisfying collection)
- Level-up screen explosion of particles
- Weapon upgrade visual change (more duct tape, larger barrels)
- Kill counter celebration at milestones (100, 500, 1000)
